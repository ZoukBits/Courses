Beginner Courses

by ZoukBits

We design our courses for beginners from many different backgrounds. Our aim is to open the door into the Zouk community of Munich, while giving everyone space for their own ideas and personal growth. Alongside solid technique, each course therefore comes with a special focus — a small extra that encourages you to explore Zouk your own way.

We currently offer two courses and a practice session:

  • 17.30–18.30: Zouk in a Nutshell – overview of core concepts in Brazilian Zouk.
  • 18.30–19.00: Praktika – Social Dancing & Training, free for everyone!
  • 19.00–20.00: Zouk Social 101 – having fun at socials from the very beginning.

How to join

  1. Pick your course(s)
  2. Just come, no registration needed. You'll find us here
  3. Pay in person (Cash or PayPal)

Missed the start of a course? No problem! All our courses are drop-in.

Questions? contact us at zoukbits@zoukmunich.com
